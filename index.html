Our Story

Welcome to Bouquet By Her! Bouquet BY HER started with one simple thought — What if a bouquet could stay as beautiful as the day you received it? Real flowers fade in two days. The feeling they give shouldn't. So she started twisting, shaping and curling every single petal by hand. No water. No wilting. No rush to enjoy it before it dies. Every flower here is handmade from soft chenille. Every daisy, lily and lotus is bent slowly, every bead is placed carefully, every bow is tied with love. Because a bouquet is never just flowers. It's a sorry. It's a thank you. It's a happy birthday. It's a I was thinking of you. And now, that feeling lasts forever. Made slowly, by her, for you.
